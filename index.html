01 / About

Intelligent and sustainable maritime engineering.

I am a naval architect, marine engineer, and postdoctoral researcher at Delft University of Technology, committed to intelligent and sustainable advances in the maritime industry. My research spans autonomous vessel navigation, safe ship operation, and modular control systems that can adapt to changing missions and faults.

I earned my PhD at TU Delft in 2024 with a dissertation on safe and resilient control for marine power and propulsion plants. Before that, I studied Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering at the National Technical University of Athens.
